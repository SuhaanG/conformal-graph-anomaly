# Observed-exposure removal experiment (frozen before execution)

Date: 2026-09-23. Exploratory follow-up to external manuscript feedback.

Reuse all Amazon and Tolokers cached attribute-only and graph-feature supervised scores, ten training seeds and five existing stratified test splits per seed. Training and split seeds are unchanged. Reveal non-test deployment labels with probabilities 0.25, 0.50, and 1.00, nested by the existing revelation stream. Known training labels also identify anomalous neighbors. Exposure is the fraction of a node's graph neighbors with a known anomalous label. Unobserved labels, including every test label, are unavailable to this rule.

Remove fractions 0, .05, .10, .20, .40, .60, .80, .90, .97 of the revealed-normal reference pool, retaining ceil((1-fraction)*pool_size) nodes. In the exposure arm retain the lowest-exposure nodes, breaking equal-exposure ties by a fresh uniform ordering per calibration repetition. The comparator retains an equally large uniform random subset. Retain the entire remaining budget; there is no 1,000-node cap. Both arms use identical test nodes, scorers, reference counts, and score tie handling. Fifty paired calibration repetitions per condition. Exposure ties and reference draws are shared across scorers and removal fractions; score ties are ordered once by independent per-node continuous uniforms, fixed before evaluation splits, ensuring distinct lexicographic scores without changing strict score order.

BH level is .10. Retain every outcome including abstention. Save every trial as compressed CSV and split-level summaries; first average repetitions and splits within training seed, then report mean and sample SD across ten seed averages. Seed SD is not new-graph uncertainty. Record paired FDP differences and fraction of nonempty outcomes. Mild-selection results (.05, .10, .20) are reported whether positive or negative. No selection rule, detector setting, or removal fraction is chosen using test FDP. This grid is a sensitivity study, not evidence that these thresholds are used in practice.

Validation: no train/calibration/test overlap; reference labels normal; unknown-label perturbation leaves exposure unchanged; matched n and identical zero-removal outcomes; deterministic seeds; optimized score-ordered BH agrees with the independent reference implementation on random tied-score examples. No GPU training is required.
